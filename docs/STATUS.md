Tire-wear update (Claude): second physics feature, `tire_wear`, OFF by default and switched separately from `tire_temperature`. While ON, `acng_tires` reads BeamNG's own per-wheel slip power (`slipEnergy`) and wears the tread: 7.5 MJ of slip energy takes a tire from new to bald at rate 1, a tire above the grip window wears up to 2x faster, and grip falls linearly to 85 % when bald. Final grip is heat grip x wear grip. Switching one part while the other stays on reconfigures without a reload, so tread carries over; reset fits fresh tires.
